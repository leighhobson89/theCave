**Black Pine Detachment – District of Saskatchewan, North-West Territories**

# MISSING PERSON INVESTIGATION - PROGRESS REPORT – THREE MONTHS

**Case No.:** 01-1210-00
**Date:** 12 October 1901

**Location:** Black Pine Former Silver Mine (B Shaft)

---

### Persons Involved

**Andrew John Spencer DOB 12 AUG 1888 (12)**
Recovered alive. Died from injuries sustained.

**Arnold Askew Spencer DOB 06 NOV 1891 (9) Known as 'Arnie'**
Missing. Presumed DEAD.

---

### Summary

Both boys entered the abandoned B Shaft of the Black Pine Former Silver Mine during the afternoon of 27 July 1901.

Andrew John Spencer later returned to Spencer Farm, Black Pine, suffering severe injuries according to witness statements. He later died at the residence from suspected blood loss and shock, confirmed by the local Coroner on 28 July.

The search for Arnold Askew Spencer continues.

---

### Evidence Recovered

• White cotton handkerchief
• Wooden varnished lunchbox
• 40 ft hemp rope
• Unlit straw torch saturated with fuel oil

---

**Case Status** OPEN – MISSING PERSON PRESUMED DEAD

**Remarks** 

No explanation has been established for the circumstances surrounding Andrew John Spencer's injuries.
